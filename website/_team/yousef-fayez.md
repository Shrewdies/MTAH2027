Yousef Fayez is the squad's professional football analyst and its football authority. Everything The
Shrewdies build starts, and ends, with his reading of the match.

## The scout's eye

Scouting is pattern recognition under pressure: a player seen in many contexts and judged against a role
rather than a reputation. The discipline lies in telling a full-back who is out of position from a
full-back who was told to be there, and in knowing how often a single clip lies. Yousef brings that
discipline to a team of engineers, who need it most when a model looks confident.

## Game analysis as a craft

Professional game analysis is less about watching more and more about watching with a question. Phases
of play, pressing triggers, rest defence, the moment a team gives the ball away on purpose: Yousef frames
these as the questions a coach would ask. That is exactly the form a model needs before it can learn
anything useful, and exactly the form its answers must take before a coach will read them.

## The interpreter

Alongside analysis, Yousef has worked as an interpreter in professional football clubs. It is an
underrated skill. A tactical instruction loses its edge when it is translated word for word; it has to
arrive with its intent intact, at the speed of a half-time talk. He works in Arabic, English, Spanish and
Portuguese: four football cultures in one voice.

## Supervision

Within The Shrewdies, Yousef is the realistic perspective. He reviews outputs, challenges assumptions and
keeps the work tied to what clubs actually use. The engineers on the squad can build almost anything; his
job is to say which of those things a coach would trust on a Monday morning.
