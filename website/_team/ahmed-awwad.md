Ahmed Awwad wears number 3 and plays the position that connects everything: the navigator. His work sits
where moving things meet maps and networks, from self-driving cars to the cellular grid that carries
their data.

## Autonomy and perception

His experience in self-driving cars matters directly to football. An autonomous vehicle has to locate
itself, predict every moving object around it and keep going when a sensor loses sight of something. A
tracking system on a pitch faces the same three problems, with a ball that vanishes behind bodies for long
stretches. Ahmed reads those gaps as engineering problems with known families of solutions, not as noise.

## Geospatial thinking

Much of his work is spatial: where demand sits on a map, where capacity runs out and where the next cell
site should go. One of his patents covers exactly that choice. On a 105 by 68 metre pitch the same
instincts become zones, densities and passing lanes.

## Telecom at national scale

At DISH he worked on the National RF team, where colleagues credit him with capital-planning models, and
a recommendation describes him as manager of traffic and capacity planning. He then spent three years at
EchoStar managing an engineering team on a 5G network. His patent portfolio, more than twenty patents,
covers capacity and congestion detection in open radio access networks, network performance,
call-failure resolution, cell-site planning and traffic balancing. He has also published a quantified analysis of how
over-the-top services affect mobile operators.

## Cloud and leadership

Today he is with Amazon Web Services in Denver. His certifications trace the arc: PMP in 2020, AWS Cloud
Practitioner and Microsoft Data Analyst Associate in 2021, AWS Certified AI Practitioner in 2026. His
professional journeys run through twelve countries, from Japan and the Philippines to Norway, Mexico and
Oman, and he works in Arabic, English and Spanish.

For The Shrewdies he brings a leader's habit: turning a hard technical picture into a plan a large team
can execute.
