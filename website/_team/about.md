We are **The Shrewdies**, a squad of four entering More Than a Hack 2027. Between us we bring five
cultures and professional journeys through nearly twenty countries. One of us analyses football for a living;
the other three build the kind of systems football analysis now depends on: perception for moving
objects, maps and networks, cloud platforms, and AI that runs with little supervision. We read possession
as a football question first and an engineering question second. Read why that mix matters in
[Why Our Specialization Matters]({{ '/team/why-our-specialization-matters/' | relative_url }}).

## How we work

- **One question per decision.** Every non-obvious choice is written down as a
  [Architecture study]({{ '/architecture/' | relative_url }}).
- **Evidence before tuning.** We read the data and the metric code before writing model code; see the
  [Initial Challenge]({{ '/challenges/initial-challenge/' | relative_url }}).
- **The analyst signs off.** Outputs are checked against a professional analyst's reading of the match.
- **Reproducible by default.** Everything runs in one pinned container on a laptop CPU.
